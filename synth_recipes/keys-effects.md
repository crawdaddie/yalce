# Keys, strings and effects: parts 45–63

Reviewed the repository text of every part below. Parts **52–63 are truncated** in this mirror: introductory text survives, but much of the promised patch detail does not. Full original text was additionally checked for parts 52–54, 57, 59, 61–62; links below identify those sources. Linked diagrams were not inspected. Implementations are basic approximations, not hardware preset transcriptions.

Both recipes accept frequency in Hz and gate duration in seconds. Use positive durations and ordinary musical pitches (approximately 55–1760 Hz). `tonewheel` takes the played 8-foot pitch: 440 Hz produces components at 220, 440 and 660 Hz. Release tails are 0.3 s for `string_pad` and 0.015 s for `tonewheel`.

The string layers, filtering and stereo spread follow `../strings.ylc`; attack/release are shortened to 0.08/0.3 s. Gains and other settings are demonstration choices. They are not transcribed hardware presets. Neither recipe claims acoustic realism; the organ omits shared wheel phases, foldback, leakage, percussion and rotary processing.

Additional recipes live in `../synth-recipes/winds.ylc` and `../synth-recipes/effects.ylc`. `clarinet` follows part 24’s odd-harmonic low-register model. Wind voices take `freq duration`; releases: pan pipe/clarinet/violin 0.12 s, flute 0.15 s. They work best in their musical registers, rather than across the entire keyboard.

Effects accept audio signals inside `@Audio` graphs: `chorus rate input`, `flanger rate input`, `rotary rate input`, `echo input`, `short_room input`. Rates are Hz; try 0.8 for chorus/flanger and 0.8 or 6 for rotary. They are mono, keep running, and must be stopped by their owner. Leave effect tails outside a voice’s `kill_on_end` envelope. Room/echo are clean digital approximations, not BBD, tape or measured-room models.
